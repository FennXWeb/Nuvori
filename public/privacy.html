NUVORI · YOUR ADVENTURE, YOUR DATA

Privacy & data

Updated September 27, 2026

Nuvori is a browser creature-collection game maintained through the FennXWeb/Nuvori project. This page describes the data used by the current early-access game.

Guest play and this device

Guest play stores your adventure and sound preference in your browser's local storage. It does not upload your guest adventure to the cloud. Account sessions and a local copy of each account's adventure are also stored on the device. Signing out ends the session but does not erase those local adventure copies. You can export an adventure in Settings, or remove local data through your browser's site-data controls.

Google and Discord accounts

When you choose Google or Discord sign-in, that provider shares your account identifier, email address, and basic profile information, such as your name and avatar, with Nuvori's Supabase authentication service. This information establishes your game account and associates it with your save. Nuvori requests basic sign-in information; it does not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, Discord messages, or Discord servers. Your Google or Discord password is entered only with that provider.

Cloud saves

Signed-in adventures are saved in Nuvori's Supabase project. Saves contain your chosen keeper name, pronouns and colors; your Nuvo, items and progression; your in-game location; and save timestamps. Cloud saves support restoring your adventure across devices. Database access rules limit a player's access to their own save. Project administrators and infrastructure providers can process data as needed to operate and maintain the service.

What other players see

While you are signed in and connected, the shared world sends your chosen keeper name, outfit, in-game location and movement, lead Nuvo and its variant, and temporary greeting to other connected players. It also transmits a game-account identifier to distinguish players. It does not send your email address, provider profile picture, chosen pronouns, or complete save through multiplayer presence. Choose a keeper name you are comfortable sharing.

Hosting and service providers

GitHub Pages serves the game. Supabase provides authentication, database storage and multiplayer connections. Google or Discord processes the sign-in you select. These services may process connection information, including IP addresses and operational logs, under their own policies. The game code contains no advertising or third-party analytics integration.

Your controls and deletion requests

You can play as a guest, sign out to stop sharing your multiplayer presence, export your adventure, clear this site's browser storage, and revoke Nuvori's access in Google or Discord account settings. Revoking provider access or clearing browser storage does not automatically delete your existing cloud account or save. Cloud data remains until removed from the project; no automatic deletion schedule is currently implemented.

To ask about your data or request deletion of a cloud account and its save, contact contact@fennx.org. Include the sign-in provider and account email so the request can be matched to the correct account. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or access tokens. Infrastructure logs and backups are also subject to the service providers' retention rules.
